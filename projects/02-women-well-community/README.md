# Project 02 — Women Well Community (WWC)

**AI-Assisted Infographic Design | Community Health Communication | Information Design**

**Project Status:** Completed — Portfolio Edition  
**Location:** Singapore  
**Target Audience:** Women aged 25–55, including working women and women managing household or caregiving responsibilities.

## 1. Project Overview

Women Well Community (WWC) is a proposed six-week community-based preventive health initiative exploring practical wellbeing habits, stress awareness, and peer support.

The project began as a community programme proposal developed with ChatGPT assistance. The original proposal was submitted for consideration but has not received a confirmed response or approval.

For this creative portfolio, the visual materials were revised and generalised to remove organisation-specific and neighbourhood-specific references.

The portfolio presents two AI-assisted infographic design approaches to the same programme concept.

## 2. Creative Objective

Translate a community health programme proposal into an accessible, visually engaging one-page communication format.

The design aims to communicate:

- The proposed programme's purpose
- The intended participants
- Key wellbeing objectives
- A practical six-week community pilot concept
- The programme model: Awareness → Practical Action → Peer Support

## 3. Design A — Illustrated Community Infographic

![WWC Illustrated Infographic](wwc-illustrated.png)

**Visual Direction:** Pastel purple, friendly illustrations, rounded information panels, and floral accents.

**Design Evaluation:** This approach creates a warm and approachable presentation. It may be more suitable for introducing the programme concept to potential community participants.

## 4. Design B — Professional Proposal Infographic

![WWC Professional Infographic](wwc-professional.png)

**Visual Direction:** Teal and white colour palette, structured content panels, lifestyle imagery, and professional typography.

**Design Evaluation:** This approach emphasises information hierarchy and a formal presentation style. It may be more suitable for communicating the proposal to programme reviewers or organisational stakeholders.

## 5. Visual Design Comparison

| Design Dimension | Illustrated Version | Professional Version |
|---|---|---|
| Visual Style | Friendly illustration | Professional editorial layout |
| Colour Palette | Lavender, pink, soft green | Teal, blue, white |
| Communication Tone | Warm and approachable | Structured and informative |
| Visual Emphasis | Emotional connection | Information hierarchy |
| Potential Audience | Community participants | Programme reviewers |
| Design Consideration | Dense text may affect readability | Small text requires checking at reduced sizes |

These are retrospective design evaluations, not claims that the two versions were originally commissioned for separate audiences.
