# Prompt Engineering — Women Well Community (WWC)

**Project:** AI-Assisted Community Health Infographic Design  
**Tool:** ChatGPT / OpenAI Image Generation

## 1. Original Project Brief

*Reconstructed from earlier project materials. This is a summary of the creative requirements, not a verbatim copy of the first ChatGPT prompt.*

Develop a visual communication concept for Women Well Community (WWC), a proposed six-week preventive-health community programme in Singapore.

The intended audience includes women aged 25–55 who work full-time or part-time, as well as women managing household and caregiving responsibilities.

The proposed programme combines three elements:

- **Awareness:** Accessible health and wellbeing discussions.
- **Practical Action:** Small, manageable steps towards healthier routines.
- **Peer Support:** Community connections and encouragement.

The infographic should communicate the programme purpose, intended audience, proposed activities and overall approach in a clear and approachable format.

## 2. Visual Direction A — Illustrated Community Infographic

*Summary of the later AI image-generation instructions used for the public portfolio version.*

Create a single-page vertical infographic poster using a friendly, pastel illustration style.

**Visual requirements:**
- Lavender, pink and soft-green colour palette
- Watercolour-like backgrounds
- Rounded information panels
- Friendly illustrations of women
- Floral, heart and wellbeing motifs
- Clear headings and readable sans-serif typography
- Organised information hierarchy

The design should feel welcoming and accessible to community participants.

Remove organisation-specific names, logos and neighbourhood references for public portfolio use.

## 3. Visual Direction B — Professional Proposal Infographic

*Summary of the later AI image-generation instructions used for the public portfolio version.*

Create a professional vertical infographic presenting the same proposed programme.

**Visual requirements:**
- Calm teal and white colour palette
- Structured information panels
- Modern sans-serif typography
- Soft wave accents
- Lifestyle photography featuring Asian women
- Clear visual hierarchy and consistent spacing

The design should communicate the proposal in a professional, informative and accessible manner.

Remove organisation-specific names, logos and neighbourhood references for public portfolio use.

## 4. Portfolio Revision Requirements

The later public portfolio versions were revised to:

- Generalise the location to Singapore.
- Remove references that might imply institutional affiliation.
- Retain the proposed six-week pilot format.
- Present the programme as a concept rather than an implemented intervention.
- Correct wording and review the consistency of programme information.
- Preserve two independent visual approaches for comparison.

ChatGPT supported programme content development and AI image generation.

My contributions included developing the programme concept, providing visual references and creative requirements, reviewing generated designs, and selecting the versions presented in this portfolio.
