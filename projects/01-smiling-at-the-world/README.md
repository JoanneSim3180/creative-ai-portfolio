# Project 01 — Smiling at the World

**AI-Assisted Album Cover Design | Creative Direction | Visual Storytelling**

**Chinese Title:** 微笑着看世界  
**Creative Theme:** Nostalgic Nanyang Watercolour  
**Project Status:** Initial Visual Concept Completed

## 1. Project Overview

*Smiling at the World* (微笑着看世界) is an AI-assisted album cover concept exploring childhood wonder, warmth, optimism, and nostalgic Southeast Asian scenery.

The artwork combines watercolour illustration, tropical flowers, heritage architecture, and a sunset coastal landscape to create a gentle, uplifting atmosphere.

## 2. Visual Concept

The artwork features a young girl overlooking a colourful coastal town, surrounded by tropical flowers, traditional architecture, and whimsical elements.

Key visual elements include:

- Warm pastel colours and watercolour textures
- Nanyang-inspired heritage architecture
- Tropical hibiscus and bougainvillea
- A youthful character looking towards the horizon
- Whimsical clouds and a decorative kite
- Expressive Chinese calligraphy-style typography

## 3. Creative Direction

The visual concept explores the relationship between childhood imagination, nostalgia, and emotional storytelling.

The illustration uses a warm colour palette and an open landscape composition to communicate a sense of hope and curiosity.

## 4. AI-Assisted Design Process

**Tool:** OpenAI Image Generation

The initial artwork was created using generative AI.

This case study documents the selected visual concept. The original generation prompt and any further design iterations will be added if their records are available.

## 5. Design Evaluation

**Strengths**

- Recognisable nostalgic Southeast Asian visual elements
- Cohesive watercolour illustration style
- Warm and emotionally expressive atmosphere
- Clear visual hierarchy between the title and illustration

**Areas for Further Development**

- Review Chinese typography for readability and accuracy
- Evaluate composition at smaller album-thumbnail sizes
- Refine the design for potential digital music distribution
