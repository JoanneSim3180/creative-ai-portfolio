# Prompt Engineering — Smiling at the World

**Project:** AI-Assisted Album Cover Design  
**Artwork:** 微笑着看世界 (Smiling at the World)  
**AI Tool:** ChatGPT / OpenAI Image Generation  
**Project Status:** Completed — Final Artwork  
**Creative Direction & Documentation:** Joanne Sim

## 1. Creative Objective

Create an emotionally engaging album cover for women aged 25–48, drawing on the warmth and approachable emotional tone associated with Fish Leong's album artwork.

The project explores a nostalgic Nanyang-inspired visual direction, using existing creative drafts as references.

## 2. Original Prompt

The following is the original user instruction provided to ChatGPT:

> I need a album cover. target audience 25-48 yo woman, something like fish leong album style. 这是我之前draft的南洋风味、

### Supporting Visual References

Existing Nanyang-inspired visual drafts were provided alongside the prompt.

### Creative Brief Interpretation

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Deliverable | Album cover design |
| Target Audience | Women aged 25–48 |
| Artistic Reference | Fish Leong's approachable, emotionally expressive album aesthetic |
| Cultural Direction | Nanyang-inspired visual storytelling |
| Starting Materials | Existing visual drafts |

The original prompt was concise, relying on the creative brief and supplied visual references rather than detailed technical instructions.

## 3. AI-Assisted Creative Workflow

**Step 1 — Define the Creative Brief**

Establish the intended audience, album cover format, artistic reference, and Nanyang-inspired visual direction.

**Step 2 — Provide Visual References**

Supply existing creative drafts to communicate the desired cultural and aesthetic direction.

**Step 3 — Generate the Artwork**

Use ChatGPT / OpenAI Image Generation to produce an illustrated album cover based on the brief and references.

**Step 4 — Review the Visual Result**

Evaluate the generated artwork for its emotional tone, visual coherence, cultural atmosphere, and suitability as an album cover.

**Step 5 — Final Artwork Selection**

Select the completed artwork, *Smiling at the World*, for presentation in the portfolio.

The creator also reported using ChatGPT / AI for Chinese title lettering adjustments. The exact sequence of these adjustments has not been documented.

## 4. Visual Characteristics of the Final Artwork

The completed artwork features:

- Watercolour-inspired illustration
- Soft blue, peach, yellow, and green tones
- A young female character wearing a blue gingham dress
- Southeast Asian heritage-inspired architecture
- Tropical flowers and coastal scenery
- A decorative kite and whimsical clouds
- Chinese calligraphy-inspired title lettering

These are observations of the final artwork, not claims that each visual element was explicitly specified in the original prompt.

## 5. Creative Evaluation

The final artwork communicates a warm, nostalgic atmosphere through its colour palette, illustration style, and Southeast Asian-inspired environment.

The combination of a youthful character, heritage architecture, and whimsical decorative elements supports the intended emotional storytelling.

The completed artwork was selected for portfolio presentation as the final design.

## 6. AI Transparency

The artwork was generated using ChatGPT / OpenAI Image Generation, informed by the creator's original brief and supplied visual references.

The creator provided the creative direction, evaluated the generated result, and selected the final artwork.

AI-generated visual elements are distinguished from the creator's documented creative input and subsequent evaluation.
